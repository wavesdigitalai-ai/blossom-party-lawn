The Grand Arrival

Our Story ✿

Born from a simple idea — celebrations deserve open skies

In the fast-growing north-west of Ahmedabad, between Gota and New Science City Road, Blossom Party Lawn was shaped as a garden escape for the city's big days — a lawn where the breeze sets the pace and the evenings glow gold.

Over the years, our courts and greens have hosted pheras under floral mandaps, nine-night garba marathons, dealer galas and first-birthday picnics — each staged with the same quiet standard: guests arrive to grandeur, and leave already talking about the food.

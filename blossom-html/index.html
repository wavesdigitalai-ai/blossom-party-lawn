The Venue ✿ Ognaj, Ahmedabad

A garden wedding venue that feels serenely yours

Good to Know ✿

Questions families always ask us

The lush outdoor lawn comfortably hosts 250–600 guests depending on your chosen seating: floating, round-table or theatre-style arrangements.
Off New Science City Road at Vasantnagar Township, Ognaj, Ahmedabad 380060 — minutes from Gota and the S P Ring Road, with easy drives from Chandkheda, Motera and central Ahmedabad.
Events and celebrations run from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM — ideal for haldi mornings through to reception evenings.

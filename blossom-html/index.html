The Venue ✿ Ognaj, Ahmedabad

A garden wedding venue that feels serenely yours

A few minutes from Gota — just off S P Ring Road at Ognaj — 1.5+ acres of manicured lawn, palm-lined courts and a grand paved arrival plaza set the stage for celebrations under open skies.

  • Flexible layouts — mandap, stage & LED wall, round-table or lounge seating

Questions families always ask us
